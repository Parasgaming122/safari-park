Introduction

Karachi Safari Park is a prominent recreational and public facility that provides visitors with access to green spaces, natural landscapes, wildlife, and a range of recreational amenities. Maintaining its cleanliness, vegetation, and natural environment is essential to ensuring a healthy, aesthetically pleasing, and sustainable setting for both visitors and wildlife.

The Environment and Sustainability Project aims to make a practical contribution to the improvement and long-term maintenance of Safari Park. Its objectives are to enhance cleanliness, strengthen the park’s green spaces, improve the visual quality of public areas, and promote the responsible use and preservation of shared facilities.

To achieve these objectives, the project will focus on three principal activities:

1. Pond cleaning: Accumulated waste will be removed to improve the condition, cleanliness, and appearance of the pond area.
2. Cleaning and plantation in food-stall and track areas: These areas will be cleaned and supplemented with suitable plantation to reduce litter, enhance their visual appeal, and strengthen the park’s greenery.
3. Assessment of plantation gaps: Areas with insufficient vegetation will be identified, and suitable locations for additional plantation will be recommended to improve the park’s overall green cover.

Collectively, these activities are intended to create a direct and sustainable impact by improving cleanliness, supporting healthier green spaces, and encouraging responsible waste disposal, plantation, and public facility use. Through this project, we seek to contribute to a cleaner, greener, and more sustainable environment while promoting environmental awareness and shared responsibility for preserving public spaces.
